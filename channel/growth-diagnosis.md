# Why the first Zorb Shorts got few views (2026-11-02)

## Data
- 3 Zorb Shorts posted 2026-11-01; about 3 views total 10-16 h later. All public (checked).
- Channel: 5 subscribers. Older views came from Ronaldo clips (about 1,300 each).
- YouTube analytics lag 2-3 days, so there is no retention data yet. This diagnosis comes from comparing the videos with a winning finance Short.

## Benchmark: Primate Economics "Monkey Explains Tax" (116k views, 49 s)
- Says what it is in the first word: "Types of taxes."
- A new item and a new picture about every 2.5 s, for 20 items in 49 s.
- One simple metaphor (leaves = money), no long numbers read aloud.
- No intro, no outro.

## What was wrong with ours
1. **First second:** a dark, static scene with a small Zorb and a small caption. Nothing says what the video is about, so the feed swipes it away.
2. **Slow pictures:** about 6 s per scene, with little motion.
3. **Dead outro:** a 3 s sign-off every time. Viewers leave there, and the video never loops.
4. **Titles start with "Zorb":** nobody searches that name.
5. **Long numbers read aloud,** like "3,155.02 dollars". The robot voice makes them slow.
6. **No reason to comment.**
7. **Wrong first audience (channel history):** YouTube first shows a new Short to people like the channel's past viewers. Here those are football fans, and they swipe on finance.

## What changed in the pipeline
- **Hook card:** the topic's punch text is huge on screen from frame 0.
- **Motion:** a push-in on every scene and a pop transition on every beat. Zorb is bigger and bobs.
- **Captions** are bigger.
- **Faster pictures:** 8-14 short beats (at most 14 words each), so the picture changes every 2-4 s.
- **Loop:** no outro. The last line loops back into the first.
- **Titles:** lead with the topic and end with 2 hashtags.
- **Pinned comment:** every post gets a question as its first comment.
- **Numbers:** at most one spoken number per beat, and long decimals go on screen, not in the voice.

## What only the owner can do (big impact)
1. **Make the Ronaldo Shorts private** (YouTube Studio > Content > Visibility). This is the strongest fix for item 7.
2. **Check "Made for kids":** YouTube Studio > Content > each Zorb Short > Audience. Cartoons are sometimes auto-flagged as kids content, which turns off comments and most recommendations. It must say "No, it's not made for kids".
3. **Verify the channel** (youtube.com/verify, phone number). This unlocks custom Shorts covers.
4. **Cross-post to TikTok and Instagram:** connect them in Metricool and the same Shorts can go there too.
5. **Patience:** brand-new channels often get their first real test batch 24-72 h after posting. Judge each video after 48 h, not 12.
