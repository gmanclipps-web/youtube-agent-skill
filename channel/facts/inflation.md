# Inflation

- Inflation is the increase in the prices of goods and services over time: a general rise in the overall price level, not the price of one product. (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/faqs/economy_14419.htm)
- The Federal Reserve aims for inflation of 2 percent over the longer run, measured by the annual change in the PCE price index. (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/faqs/economy_14400.htm)
- "Core" inflation measures leave out food and energy because their prices go up and down a lot. (source: https://www.federalreserve.gov/faqs/economy_14419.htm)
- In the 12 months ending September 2026, the Consumer Price Index (CPI-U) rose 3.7 percent; without food and energy it rose 2.6 percent (BLS release of October 14, 2026). (source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm)
- BLS example: a 7 percent price increase is the same as a basket of goods and services that cost $100 now costing $107. (source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm)
- To build the CPI, BLS collects prices in 75 urban areas from about 6,000 housing units and about 22,000 stores and other retail places. (source: https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm)
