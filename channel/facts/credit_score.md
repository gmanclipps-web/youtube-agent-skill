# Credit score

- A credit score is a prediction of your credit behavior, such as how likely you are to pay a loan back on time, based on your credit reports. (source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/)
- Most credit scores range from 300 to 850. (source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/)
- FICO Scores range from 300 to 850 and are calculated from credit report data. (source: https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/what-is-a-fico-score)
- FICO Score weights: payment history 35 percent, amounts owed 30 percent, length of credit history 15 percent, credit mix 10 percent, new credit 10 percent. (source: https://www.myfico.com/credit-education/whats-in-your-credit-score)
- You do not have just one credit score; it depends on the scoring model, the data and even the day it was calculated. (source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/)
- Lenders use credit scores to decide whether to offer credit and to set the interest rate and credit limit you receive. (source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/)
